🌱 Mission Statement

At Placer Forest School, our mission is to create a respectful, nature-rich learning environment where children ages 4 and up are free to explore, connect, and grow. Rooted in the principles of respectful parenting, child-led discovery, and unstructured outdoor play, we offer families in the Lincoln, Roseville, and Rocklin area a forest school experience that supports whole-child development, fosters community, and honors the wonder of childhood.
