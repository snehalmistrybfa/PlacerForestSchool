Placer Forest School, LLC — Waiver & Release of Liability (Family Agreement)

This agreement applies to the parent/guardian signing below and all children listed in the enrollment form.

1. Ability to Participate & Assumption of Risk

I certify that my child(ren) are physically and emotionally able to participate in Placer Forest School, LLC’s outdoor programs. I understand outdoor learning involves natural risks such as uneven ground, natural terrain, changing weather, and exposure to plants, insects, and animals. I voluntarily assume all such risks on behalf of my child(ren) and myself and give permission for them to take part in all program activities.

2. Medical Emergency Authorization

I authorize Placer Forest School, LLC staff to provide basic first aid and to obtain emergency medical treatment for my child(ren) if I cannot be reached promptly. I agree to be responsible for all costs associated with emergency medical care and acknowledge that Placer Forest School, LLC does not provide medical insurance coverage for participants.

I hereby release, waive, and discharge Placer Forest School, LLC, its owners (Malgorzata Liro and Snehal Mistry), employees, volunteers, independent contractors, property owners, insurers, successors, and assigns from any and all liability, claims, demands, or actions arising out of participation in programs or presence at the facility.

This includes, but is not limited to, claims for personal injury, property damage, medical expenses, or loss of services, whether arising from my negligence, the negligence of third parties, or the ordinary negligence of Placer Forest School, LLC. This waiver does not release claims based on intentional misconduct or gross negligence where prohibited by law.

I covenant not to sue Placer Forest School, LLC for any claim released by this Agreement. I further agree to indemnify and defend Placer Forest School, LLC against any such claim, including reasonable attorney’s fees and costs.

4. Program Rules and Safety Agreements

I acknowledge that I have received and read the program’s rules and safety agreements and will review them with my child(ren). I agree that my child(ren) will comply with these rules as a condition of participation.

I understand that Placer Forest School, LLC is not responsible for any lost, stolen, or damaged valuables or property.

6. Tuition & Payment Agreement

I acknowledge that a non-refundable registration and supply fee must accompany this form and that my child's enrollment is not confirmed until the fee is received. I agree to pay the session tuition either in full or in two consecutive installments, starting on or before the first day of the semester. If I am using charter funds, I will provide a purchase order (P.O.) by the first day of the semester. I understand that although my charter school will be billed for the full amount, I am ultimately responsible for any portion not covered, and I agree to pay any remaining balance by the last day of the semester.

7. Nonrefundable Tuition Policy

Tuition is nonrefundable once classes start. If you need to withdraw from the program, please contact us to discuss your specific circumstances.
